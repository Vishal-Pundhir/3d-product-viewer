🎨 3D Gaussian Splat Viewer - Embed Example

This is an example of how to embed the 3D viewer in your website. Replace YOUR_SKU_ID with your actual SKU ID.

Embedding Options

  • sku_id (required) - Your SKU identifier
  • autoRotate (optional) - Enable auto rotation (default: true)
  • bg (optional) - Enable background model (default: false)
  • gradient (optional) - Enable gradient background (default: false)
  • tier (optional) - Performance tier: low, basic, medium, high, ultra-high
